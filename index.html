How to read this plan

Frames determine the physical footprint. Active LED area and resolution determine pixels. Insets are centered within each cabinet.

Choose one of nine positions for the whole layout. Two fitted grids join before positioning: left / right grids share their bottom edge; bottom / top grids share their left edge. Unequal extents form a stepped outline. Gaps measure the assembled outer frame bounds to the wall edges in mm; overflow is labelled explicitly.

Lock the composition once its size is right. The lock holds wall dimensions, cabinet choices, fit, cut and library edits; placement and exports remain available. Lock state is saved on this device. Unlock before changing the composition.

The cut sets sizing allocations for cabinet counts: from the left for a vertical cut, from the bottom for a horizontal cut. It is not a fixed seam position after packing. Exact fit compares the nearest positive integer footprint to each allocation axis, independently, with an inclusive 0.5 mm tolerance. Floor and ceil use only a floating-point roundoff guard, never that tolerance.

Utilization is summed cabinet footprint / wall area. A cover layout can exceed 100%. LED aspect ratio uses aggregate active dimensions, excluding frame gaps.

Mixed grids get a combined canvas only when their physical and pixel alignment is clean. Otherwise use processor mapping / two canvases.

Power totals use installed cabinet counts and the optional typical / average and maximum W per cabinet in your panel library. Missing ratings show unknown or partial totals. These are load estimates, not circuit or installation sizing.

Large grids simplify drawing details only. All numerical results remain unchanged.
