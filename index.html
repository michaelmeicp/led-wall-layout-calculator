How to read this plan

Frames determine the physical footprint. Active LED area and resolution determine pixels. Insets are centered within each cabinet.

Choose the center or a corner of each zone. Gaps measure from the physical cabinet frame to each zone edge in mm. Negative space is labelled overflow. Vertical cuts measure from the left; horizontal cuts measure from the bottom.

Exact fit compares the nearest positive integer footprint to each target axis, independently, with an inclusive 0.5 mm tolerance. Floor and ceil use only a floating-point roundoff guard, never that tolerance.

Utilization is summed cabinet footprint / target area; overlap is counted twice and flagged. It can exceed 100%. LED aspect ratio uses aggregate active dimensions, excluding frame gaps.

Mixed grids get a combined canvas only when their physical and pixel alignment is clean. Otherwise use processor mapping / two canvases.

Power totals use installed cabinet counts and the optional typical / average and maximum W per cabinet in your panel library. Missing ratings show unknown or partial totals. These are load estimates, not circuit or installation sizing.

Large grids simplify drawing details only. All numerical results remain unchanged.
